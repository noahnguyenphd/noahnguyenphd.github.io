Huan (Noah) Nguyen, MBA, MSc, PMP®

Huan (Noah) Nguyen is a Ph.D. student in Business Information Technology at Virginia Tech, Pamplin College of Business. He holds an M.S. in Computer Science and an MBA, and has worked in various roles in the software development industry before starting his Ph.D. His research is on the behavioral and social side of cybersecurity, including privacy, misinformation, and information assurance, using surveys, experiments, and structural equation modeling alongside computational methods such as natural language processing, machine learning, and large language models. His work has appeared in journals including Computers in Human Behavior and Technological Forecasting and Social Change, and has been presented at ICIS, PACIS, and AMCIS. He serves as an ad-hoc reviewer for Information Systems Frontiers and the International Journal of Information Management Data Insights, and as a conference reviewer for ICIS, PACIS, ECIS, AMCIS, HICSS, and ACIS.
