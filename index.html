Huan (Noah) Nguyen, MBA, MSc, PMP®

Huan (Noah) Nguyen is a Ph.D. student in Business Information Technology at Virginia Tech, Pamplin College of Business. He holds an M.S. in Computer Science, an MBA, and a PMP® certification, and has about ten years of experience in the software industry. His research is in behavioral information security. He studies why employees protect, or put at risk, the information and systems of their organizations. This research covers security policy compliance and violation, shadow IT and shadow AI, and the security governance of AI and AI agents at work. It also covers the outcomes of these behaviors for data breaches and firm performance. He also works on misinformation and information assurance, including how organizations and their information systems respond during crises. He uses surveys, experiments, interviews, econometric analysis of archival data, machine learning, and natural language processing.

His work appears in journals including Computers in Human Behavior, Technological Forecasting and Social Change, European Journal of Innovation Management, and Acta Psychologica. It also appears in Springer edited volumes and in the proceedings of ICIS, PACIS, and AMCIS. He serves as an ad hoc associate editor for the AOM Annual Meeting (CTO Division) and on the editorial board of Information Systems Research in Vietnam (Springer). He reviews for Information Systems Frontiers, Technological Forecasting and Social Change, International Journal of Information Management Data Insights, and International Journal of Management Education. He also reviews for the AOM, ICIS, PACIS, ECIS, AMCIS, HICSS, and ACIS conferences.
